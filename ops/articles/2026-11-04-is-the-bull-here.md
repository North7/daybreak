## 中文

### 標題

牛到底來了沒？我把數字攤開來看

### 內文

這幾天看到很多人在問同一件事：不是說牛市來了嗎，怎麼越走越弱？

心情我懂。從 9/21 的高點 86,594 到 11/03 收盤 77,332，跌了 10.7%。ETF 10/30 和 11/02 兩天流出 10.4 億美元，恐懼貪婪指數一週內從 48 掉到 26。價格還比一年前低 30.5%。這種時候說「牛市」，聽起來確實有點刺耳。

所以我想把這件事拆成兩個問題：第一，「牛市」到底怎麼算；第二，現在的數字裡，哪些站在牛這邊、哪些站在懷疑這邊。

**一、先講清楚「牛市」怎麼算**

我不用感覺判斷，用一條固定的規則：收盤連 30 天站在「上升中」的 200 日均線之上，才算進入牛市；要退回熊市，得收盤連 30 天落在「下降中」的 200 日均線之下。

照這條規則，這輪是 2026-10-09 判定進入牛市。到 11/03，收盤已經連 55 天在 200 日均線之上，均線本身還在往上走（現在 73,157，30 天前 71,502）。

這條規則看的是趨勢有沒有翻過來，不是看會不會漲到哪裡。它說「牛市第 26 天」，意思是趨勢已經轉向，不是說接下來一定會漲。

**二、站在「牛已經來了」這邊的數字**

- **低點已經離得很遠**：這輪的低點是 6/30 的 58,525，到現在漲了約 32%，期間最高漲到 86,594。
- **新資金還在進來，只是變慢**：實現市值（每顆幣最後一次移動時的價格加總）30 天 +0.67%，已經連 72 天是正的。
- **幣持續離開交易所**：交易所上的 BTC 供給，90 天減少 0.76%。
- **週線趨勢偏多**：我追蹤的週線多空分數從 9/21 起轉為多方，到現在 6 週。
- **離過熱很遠**：Tidemark 頂部訊號是 0；價格只有冪律趨勢價的 0.53 倍，過去四輪的底部大概在 0.24–0.48 倍，頂部從 8.49 倍一路縮到 1.12 倍。現在的位置比較靠近歷史底部那一側，不是頂部那一側。

我用 6 盞燈當佐證，現在亮 4 盞：MVRV 高於自己的一年均值、實現市值 30 天成長為正、交易所供給 90 天減少、收盤高於短期持有者成本。

**三、讓人懷疑的數字，也是真的**

懷疑不是沒有道理。現在最大的問題是：**價格有撐住，但現貨買盤沒有跟上**。

- **美國現貨在折價**：Coinbase 溢價 7 日均 −0.19%，是我從 2026 年 1 月開始記錄以來最低。美國的現貨買家比全球其他地方更不積極。
- **亞洲也沒接**：OKX 場外 USDT 對人民幣的價格比匯率低 1.14%，亞洲的買盤也偏弱。
- **穩定幣在縮**：總量 2,960 億美元，30 天少了 111 億。
- **老手在賣**：長期持有者 30 天淨減持 149,737 顆。這段淨減持從 8/4 開始已經 91 天，同期價格反而漲了 19.5%，等於老手一路趁反彈出貨。
- **短線買家在認賠**：短期持有者的 SOPR 是 0.9954，連 5 天低於 1，代表他們賣出時多半是虧的；短期持有者裡只有 32.29% 還在獲利，是 30 天來最低。
- **撐盤的是槓桿**：永續合約的加權資金費率年化 10.7%，做多的人還在付錢維持部位。

另外兩盞沒亮的燈也要誠實講：12 個月動能是 −30.5%（價格比一年前低），短期持有者成本還比真實市場均價低 4.41%。週線多空分數雖然偏多，但只有 0.88，絕對值不到 1，方向很容易反覆。

**四、那歷史上「牛市初段」長什麼樣子？**

我回頭看週線多空分數過去幾次由空翻多：

- **2015-10-05**：翻多後維持了 128 週，一年後價格 +154%。
- **2019-04-22**：翻多後只撐了 30 週就翻回空方，是典型的假突破。
- **2023-01-23**：翻多後維持了 153 週，一年後價格 +73%。

這輪是 2026-09-21 翻多。

所以我的看法是兩面的：牛市初段被懷疑很正常，2015 和 2023 初期也是一路有人說「這只是熊市反彈」。但 2019 也提醒我們，初段是有可能失敗的，沒有任何規則能事先排除這種情況。樣本只有三四輪，只能當背景，不能當保證。

**五、接下來我會看這幾件事**

1. **短線買家什麼時候不再認賠**：STH-SOPR 能不能收回 1 以上。
2. **76K–75K 這一層守不守得住**：短期持有者的平均成本在 75,192–75,892 之間，11/03 這一層一天多了 13.5 萬顆短線籌碼。守住，成本就在這裡重新墊高；失守，帳面虧損的人會一下子變多。
3. **現貨資金回不回來**：Coinbase 溢價轉正、ETF 恢復淨流入。這是現在牛市缺的那一塊。
4. **上方第一道關**：真實市場均價 79,394。
5. **兩盞還沒亮的燈**：照近 30 天的速度，短期成本大約 12/22 會上穿真實市場均價；價格持平的話，12 個月動能大約 2027-02 翻正。這兩個都是外推，不是預測。

日期上：11/6 美國非農、11/10 CPI、12/9 FOMC、12/25 Deribit 季度選擇權到期。

**結論**

照規則，牛市已經來了，今天是第 26 天。但這是一個「價格先走、現貨資金還沒跟上」的牛市初段，賣壓來自認賠的短線買家和趁反彈出貨的老手，撐盤的是槓桿。

我覺得大家懷疑一點都不奇怪。比起爭論牛來了沒，更值得盯的是：現貨買盤會不會回來。

## English

### Title

Is the bull actually here? Here's what the numbers say

### Body

I keep seeing the same question this week: if we're in a bull market, why does it feel like it's getting weaker?

Fair enough. BTC is down 10.7% from the Sep 21 high of 86,594 to a 77,332 close on Nov 3. ETFs lost $1.04B across Oct 30 and Nov 2. Fear & Greed dropped from 48 to 26 in a week. Price is still 30.5% below where it was a year ago. Calling this a bull market sounds a bit off right now.

So I split it into two questions: what counts as a bull market, and which numbers argue for it versus against it.

**1. What counts as a bull market**

I don't go by feel. I use one fixed rule: 30 straight closes above a *rising* 200-day moving average puts us in a bull phase. Going back to bear takes 30 straight closes below a *falling* 200-day MA.

By that rule, this cycle turned bull on Oct 9, 2026. As of Nov 3, we've had 55 straight closes above the 200-day MA, and the average itself is still rising (73,157 now, 71,502 thirty days ago).

The rule tells you the trend has turned. It doesn't tell you how far price goes. "Bull day 26" means the trend flipped, not that higher prices are guaranteed.

**2. The case that the bull is here**

- **The low is well behind us**: this cycle bottomed at 58,525 on Jun 30. Price is up about 32% since, with a high of 86,594 along the way.
- **New money is still coming in, just slower**: realized cap (the sum of every coin's last-moved price) is +0.67% over 30 days, positive for 72 days straight.
- **Coins keep leaving exchanges**: exchange supply is down 0.76% over 90 days.
- **The weekly trend leans bullish**: the weekly regime score I track flipped bullish on Sep 21 and has held for 6 weeks.
- **Nowhere near overheated**: the Tidemark top signal is 0. Price sits at 0.53x the power-law trend. Past cycle bottoms were roughly 0.24–0.48x, and tops have shrunk from 8.49x to 1.12x. We're much closer to the bottom side of history than the top.

I use 6 supporting signals, and 4 are on: MVRV above its 1-year average, positive 30-day realized cap growth, falling 90-day exchange supply, and price above the short-term holder cost basis.

**3. The doubts are real too**

The skepticism isn't baseless. The core issue: **price is holding, but spot buyers haven't shown up**.

- **US spot trades at a discount**: the Coinbase premium 7-day average is −0.19%, the lowest since I started tracking it in January 2026. US spot buyers are less eager than the rest of the world.
- **Asia isn't buying either**: OKX's OTC USDT/CNY price is 1.14% below the FX rate, a sign Asian demand is soft too.
- **Stablecoins are shrinking**: $296B total, down $11.1B in 30 days.
- **Long-term holders are selling**: net −149,737 BTC over 30 days. They've been net sellers for 91 days since Aug 4, while price rose 19.5%, so they've been selling into the bounce.
- **Short-term buyers are selling at a loss**: STH-SOPR is 0.9954, below 1 for 5 straight days. Only 32.29% of short-term holder supply is in profit, a 30-day low.
- **Leverage is doing the lifting**: weighted perp funding is 10.7% annualized. Longs are still paying to stay in.

Two of the supporting signals are off, and that's worth saying plainly. 12-month momentum is −30.5%, and short-term holder cost is still 4.41% below the true market mean. The weekly regime score is bullish, but only 0.88. Below 1 in absolute terms, it can flip easily.

**4. What early bull phases looked like before**

Here's what happened after past bullish flips of the weekly regime score:

- **Oct 5, 2015**: stayed bullish for 128 weeks; price +154% a year later.
- **Apr 22, 2019**: lasted just 30 weeks before flipping back. A textbook false breakout.
- **Jan 23, 2023**: stayed bullish for 153 weeks; price +73% a year later.

This cycle flipped on Sep 21, 2026.

So I see it both ways. Doubt in an early bull phase is normal; people called 2015 and 2023 a bear-market rally the whole way up. But 2019 is a reminder that early phases can fail, and no rule can rule that out ahead of time. With only three or four cycles to compare, history is context, not a guarantee.

**5. What I'm watching next**

1. **When short-term buyers stop selling at a loss**: can STH-SOPR get back above 1?
2. **Whether 76K–75K holds**: short-term holder cost basis sits at 75,192–75,892, and this band picked up 135K BTC of short-term supply on Nov 3 alone. If it holds, the cost base resets here. If it breaks, a lot more holders go underwater at once.
3. **Whether spot money comes back**: a positive Coinbase premium and ETF net inflows. That's the missing piece of this bull market.
4. **The first level overhead**: the true market mean at 79,394.
5. **The two signals still off**: at the last 30 days' pace, short-term cost crosses the true market mean around Dec 22. If price stays flat, 12-month momentum turns positive around Feb 2027. Both are extrapolations, not forecasts.

On the calendar: US payrolls Nov 6, CPI Nov 10, FOMC Dec 9, Deribit quarterly options expiry Dec 25.

**Bottom line**

By the rule, the bull is here, and today is day 26. But it's an early bull where price moved first and spot money hasn't caught up. The selling comes from short-term buyers taking losses and long-term holders selling into strength. Leverage is holding it up.

The doubt makes sense to me. Instead of arguing about whether the bull is here, I'd watch whether spot buyers come back.
