Daybreak · 比特幣多維判讀 · Bitcoin, read across every dimension

Daybreak (daybreak.aronlab.dev) is a free daily Bitcoin brief built by Aron. It updates every morning before 08:30 Taipei time, reading 134 original and public indicators across on-chain data, price structure, derivatives, fund flows and macro sentiment to answer six questions: Where we are in the cycle; Which cost bases and levels price is standing on; Has the selling been worked through; Who is buying and where the money comes from; How crowded leverage and sentiment are; So what do I do. Every chart takes a side (bullish, bearish or neutral) and says why. Code computes every number; AI only explains them, it does not calculate or decide. Daybreak makes no trade calls.

FAQ

Is Daybreak free? Do I need an account?

Yes, it is free. There is no sign-up, login, paywall or usage limit. The app, every chart page, the daily briefs and the RSS feeds are public.

Which indicators does Daybreak cover?

134 charts, including MVRV, SOPR (network, long- and short-term holders), NUPL by holder group, holder cost bases and realized price, supply distribution (URPD), exchange netflow and exchange supply, spot ETF flows, stablecoin supply, funding rates, options and implied volatility, Pi Cycle, Reserve Risk, Hash Ribbons and miners. The full list, with today's lean for each chart, is at https://daybreak.aronlab.dev/en/charts/.

Where does the data come from?

Free public sources: Bitview, CheckOnChain, CoinMetrics, Coinalyze, Binance, OKX, Bybit, Deribit, Coinbase, Kraken, Bitfinex, Hyperliquid, DefiLlama, CoinMarketCap, CFTC, FRED, alternative.me, Upbit, bitcoin-data. Each chart names its source and the date of its latest data.

How often is it updated?

Every day, from the previous UTC daily close, before 08:30 Taipei time (00:30 UTC).

How is Daybreak different from data platforms like Glassnode?

Daybreak is not a data terminal: it has no API, custom charts or raw-data downloads. It organises its charts into six questions, marks every chart bullish, bearish or neutral with a reason each day, and condenses them into one brief. For raw data, an API or custom metrics, a data platform such as Glassnode is the better fit; for a daily read-out that is already interpreted, Daybreak is the quicker option.

Does Daybreak give buy or sell signals?

No. It reports where each indicator leans and why; it makes no trade calls.

Is this Daybreak related to Daybreak Markets or daybreak.fi?

No. Daybreak at daybreak.aronlab.dev is an independent Bitcoin analysis website by Aron. It has no token and offers no trading, signals or automated trading; it is not related to other crypto projects named Daybreak.

Aron, an independent builder (Aron Lab, https://aronlab.dev/). Code computes every number; AI only writes the explanation.

Daybreak 和 Glassnode 這類資料平台有什麼不同？

Daybreak 不是資料終端：沒有 API，不能自訂圖表或下載原始資料。它把一百多張圖整理成六個問題，每天替每張圖寫出偏多、偏空或中性和理由，再收斂成一份簡報。需要原始資料、API 或自訂指標，Glassnode 這類資料平台比較合適；想每天直接看一份讀好的判讀，Daybreak 比較省事。

Daybreak 會給買賣建議嗎？

不會。它只寫每個指標偏向哪邊、為什麼，不給交易建議。

這個 Daybreak 和 Daybreak Markets、daybreak.fi 有關嗎？

無關。daybreak.aronlab.dev 的 Daybreak 是 Aron 個人做的比特幣分析網站，沒有代幣，也不提供交易、訊號或自動交易，和其他同名的加密貨幣專案沒有關係。
